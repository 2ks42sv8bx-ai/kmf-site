Install

  1. Install KOReader

    Follow KOReader's guide for your device.

  2. Add the plugin to KOReader

    Download the plugin and unzip it into KOReader's plugins folder.

  3. Connect your e-reader

    In KOReader, open Menu, Tools, vandenHove, Account, and enter the code you were given.

  4. Add the plugin to Obsidian

    Download the plugin, unzip it into your vault's .obsidian/plugins folder, then turn it on in Settings, Community plugins.

  5. Connect Obsidian

    In Obsidian, open Settings, vandenHove, and enter the code from KOReader's Account menu, under Code for another device.

  6. Sharing with a group

    On your e-reader, tap a highlight, tap the three dots (…), then Share with groups.
