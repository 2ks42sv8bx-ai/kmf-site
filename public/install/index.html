Install

Install KOReader

Follow KOReader's guide for your device.

Add the plugin to KOReader

Unzip it into KOReader's plugins folder.

Download for KOReader

Connect your e-reader

In KOReader, open Menu, Tools, vandenHove, Account, and enter the code you were given.

Add the plugin to Obsidian

Unzip it into your vault's .obsidian/plugins folder, then turn it on in Settings, Community plugins.

Download for Obsidian

Connect Obsidian

In Obsidian, open Settings, vandenHove, and enter a code.

Get one on your e-reader: Account, Code for another device.

Sharing with a group

On your e-reader, tap a highlight, then the "…" button, to share it with your groups.
