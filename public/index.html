What do you read on?

  1. Install KOReader. Steps for Kobo. Your Kindle has to be jailbroken first. Steps for Kindle. Steps for PocketBook. Also for e-readers such as BOOX. Steps for Android. Steps for reMarkable. KOReader's guide covers every device.
  2. Unzip the KOReader plugin into KOReader's plugins folder.
  3. In KOReader, open Menu, Tools, vandenHove, Account, and enter a code. Use the code you were given.
  4. Unzip the Obsidian plugin into your vault's .obsidian/plugins folder, then turn it on in Settings, Community plugins.
  5. In Obsidian, open Settings, vandenHove, and enter a code. Get one on your e-reader: Account, Code for another device.

To share a passage with your groups: on your e-reader, tap a highlight, then "…". In Obsidian, add "-shared" to its title.
